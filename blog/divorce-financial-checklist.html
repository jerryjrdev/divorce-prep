Why "marital vs. separate" matters

In community-property systems (like California and much of Spain), property acquired during the marriage is shared; property owned before the marriage, or received by gift or inheritance, is usually separate. Other systems divide by different rules — but almost everywhere, the classification matters. Tag every item as you go.
