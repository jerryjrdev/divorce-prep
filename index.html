A step-by-step plan for your finances, documents, and deadlines

Divorce is one of life's hardest transitions. Divorce Prep turns the chaos into a clear plan: a checklist built from your local family law, a vault for every asset and debt, expense tracking, settlement scenarios you can test before you sign, and a one-tap export of everything your lawyer needs.

Private by design. Everything stays on your device, locked with Face ID. No accounts. No ads. No cloud.

Everything, organized

  • Jurisdiction checklists — California, Germany, France, England & Wales, Spain, and Italy. Each checklist is built from local family law: waiting periods, required forms, deadlines.
  • Asset & debt vault — photograph, tag, and value every asset. Mark each one marital or separate. See your net worth at a glance.
  • Expense tracker — log spending as marital or separate while your case is open.
  • Settlement scenarios — test 50/50, 60/40, or any split you want. See what each of you keeps and estimated support.
  • Document hub — statements, forms, and photos in one place, exportable as a ZIP bundle for your lawyer.
  • Attorney prep — a consultation question bank, meeting notes, and court date reminders.
