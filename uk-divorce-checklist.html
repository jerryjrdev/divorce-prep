Divorce in England & Wales: The Complete Checklist

Since April 2022, divorce in England and Wales is no-fault and mostly online. Here is the full process from the D8 application to the Final Order.

How divorce works in England & Wales

Under the Divorce, Dissolution and Separation Act 2020, you no longer need to prove fault or wait two years. A sole application (one spouse applies) or a joint application (both apply together) states that the marriage has irretrievably broken down. That statement cannot be contested.

The process has a mandatory 20-week reflection period and a final 6-week-and-1-day waiting step.

The England & Wales divorce checklist

  1. Check eligibility — one of you must be habitually resident or domiciled in England or Wales.
  2. Gather details — marriage certificate, full names, addresses, and your spouse's email for service.
  3. File the D8 application online via the HMCTS portal and pay the fee (a court fee remission is available on low income).
  4. Acknowledgment — for a sole application, your spouse has 14 days to acknowledge service.
  5. Wait through the 20-week reflection period — time to consider reconciliation and sort finances.
  6. Apply for the Conditional Order — the court confirms you are entitled to divorce.
  7. Sort the finances — division of assets is separate from the divorce itself. A consent order (with a Form D81 statement of information) makes your financial settlement legally binding; a clean break ends future claims.
  8. Wait 6 weeks and 1 day after the Conditional Order.
  9. Apply for the Final Order — the divorce is now final.
  10. Update your records — banks, pensions, wills, and insurance.
