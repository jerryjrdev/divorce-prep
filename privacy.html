Privacy Policy

Private by design — everything stays on your device.

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Divorce Prep stores all data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, store, or have access to any information you enter into the app.

What we collect: nothing

There are no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no tracking pixels, and no accounts. The app does not connect to any server.

Where your data lives

All checklists, assets, expenses, documents, and notes are stored on your device only. Data leaves your device only when you explicitly export or share it yourself — for example, the JSON backup file or the lawyer export bundle. You choose where those files go.

Deleting the app deletes your data

Uninstalling Divorce Prep removes all data stored by the app. There is no copy anywhere else, because no copy ever leaves your device.

Biometric authentication

Face ID and Touch ID authentication is performed by the operating system. Biometric data never reaches the app and is never stored by the app.

Children

This app is intended for adults managing their own legal and financial affairs.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/jerryjrdev/divorce-prep or use the contact form on the support page.
